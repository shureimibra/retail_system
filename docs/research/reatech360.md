# Research: Reatech360 (features only)

- **Source:** public homepage https://reatech360.com/, captured 22 Oct 2026 with REA in an isolated headless browser. No sign-in, no private pages.
- **Rule from the owner:** borrow only what this system needs, nothing else, and keep it simple. Never borrow Reatech360's look, branding or code.

## Borrowed

| Reatech360 feature | What we take | Phase |
|---|---|---|
| Payments by M-Pesa, card or bank; the balance updates itself | A **Reserved** item records deposits (amount, method, reference) and shows the balance due. Paid in full = Recovered | 1c (data), 3 (screen) |
| Role-based access; each business's data kept separate | Owner / Manager / Cashier roles; every record belongs to one shop | 1a |

## Not borrowed (not needed now)

A customer profile with balances and history, a guided setup wizard, the AI assistant, invoicing, payroll and HR, projects, the call centre, the web studio, multi-currency, and the visual design.
